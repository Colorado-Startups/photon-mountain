### What both passes confirm about the method

Three findings hold across all 58 rows. They are the reason to run this row by row and not as a date refresh.

1. **Overclaims outnumbered stale facts.** Across both passes, more corrections were things that were never right than things that had changed. A freshness check would have caught none of them. The source was not out of date; the row said more than the source did.
2. **An ownership label is the most error-prone cell in the table.** Two of the two passes' flat errors were corporate-tie labels, Colorado Thin Films' guessed ESCO affiliation and EMI's missing Rex-Hide parent. Both came from treating silence as independence. A company's own site not naming an owner is not evidence that it has none.
3. **Famous rows are not safe rows.** The 17 rows held back as lowest-risk produced a higher correction rate than the 41 checked first. Size and stability protect the claim that a company exists; they do nothing for the claim about what it does in Colorado.

## How to read this

This list follows the project's honesty guardrails. Three things are labeled on purpose, because they change what a claim can say:

- **Colorado tie.** "HQ" means the company is headquartered in Colorado. "Facility" or "Office" means real Colorado operations under an out-of-state or foreign parent. "Roots only" means the company grew out of Colorado research but is now headquartered elsewhere. Do not call a facility a Colorado company without the qualifier.
- **Corridor tag.** *Core* is Boulder, Golden, Arvada, Denver. *Ring* is the greater corridor (Louisville, Lafayette, Broomfield, Westminster, Longmont). *Statewide* is everywhere else in Colorado. The user asked for Colorado companies as the core of the list, so the list is statewide; the corridor tag is a bonus filter.
- **Stage.** Photonic compute is emerging, not shipping at scale. Where a company is pre-revenue, a prototype, or a facility still being built, the row says so.

Several companies do more than one thing with light (for example Vescent, Octave Photonics, QPIC). Each is listed once under its primary category, with cross-tags noted.

## Method and honesty notes

Compiled by seven parallel research sweeps (solar, photonic chips, lasers and fusion, sensors, photonic quantum, components, institutions and cluster). Each sweep was instructed not to invent companies, to attach a source to every entry, and to flag HQ-versus-facility and operating-versus-planned. Acquisitions and renames are noted inline. Ball is now BAE. Maxar Intelligence is now Vantor. REO is now Excelitas Boulder. Precision Photonics and AT Films are now IDEX Advanced Thin Films. Boulder Nonlinear Systems is now inside Meadowlark. L3Harris Geospatial is now NV5. Three honesty guardrails were held throughout: the Arvada PIC fab is opening, not long-operating; NLR is "formerly NREL" on first reference; and CPIA figures are the association's figures, attributed. Insight LiDAR and Alpine Research Optics were spot-confirmed during the compile pass (the latter owned by Altechna of Lithuania since 2023). A PT-5 freshness pass on 2026-07-06 updated the Xcimer, Marvel Fusion, and CPIA rows and added attribution cues.

**The row-by-row re-verification is done.** It ran on 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-08 and covered all 58 rows, which closes the pre-publication step this section used to name as outstanding. The Imatest URL caveat that stood here is closed too, confirmed live 2026-10-08. Three things still gate external use, and they are specific rather than general: the ASD Boulder site status, the Areté Longmont-or-Niwot address, and the Lever Photonics entity status.
