# Colorado Photonics Companies (Computing-With-Light Corridor)

*Working directory of Colorado companies that build with light. Compiled 2026-06-23 for Photon Mountain. Every entry was verified against company sites, the Colorado Photonics Industry Association directory, government sources, or credible news. Sources are linked per row.*

## How to read this

This list follows the project's honesty guardrails. Three things are labeled on purpose, because they change what a claim can say:

- **Colorado tie.** "HQ" means the company is headquartered in Colorado. "Facility" or "Office" means real Colorado operations under an out-of-state or foreign parent. "Roots only" means the company grew out of Colorado research but is now headquartered elsewhere. Do not call a facility a Colorado company without the qualifier.
- **Corridor tag.** *Core* is Boulder, Golden, Arvada, Denver. *Ring* is the greater corridor (Louisville, Lafayette, Broomfield, Westminster, Longmont). *Statewide* is everywhere else in Colorado. The user asked for Colorado companies as the core of the list, so the list is statewide; the corridor tag is a bonus filter.
- **Stage.** Photonic compute is emerging, not shipping at scale. Where a company is pre-revenue, a prototype, or a facility still being built, the row says so.

Several companies do more than one thing with light (for example Vescent, Octave Photonics, QPIC). Each is listed once under its primary category, with cross-tags noted.
