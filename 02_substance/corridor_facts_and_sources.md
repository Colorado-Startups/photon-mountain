# Corridor facts and sources

The anchor institutions and assets behind Photon Mountain, by city. Carried over from Colorado AI's research, which was fact-checked there. **Re-verify every line against a current source before any external use**; this is a working reference, not cleared copy. Keep the honesty guardrails throughout.

## The core (Boulder, Golden, Arvada, Denver)

- **Boulder, the research anchor.** NIST and JILA light science and metrology (the optical frequency comb, among the most precise atomic clocks on Earth), and CU Boulder. An operating photonic-chip cleanroom is here (the NIST Boulder microfabrication facility). Frame it as operating today.
- **Arvada, the fab.** A photonic-integrated-circuit fab tied to the Quantum Commons and the Elevate Quantum ecosystem. Frame its status precisely (opening or ramping in 2026); do not imply it is at volume before it is. The first phase, including the fab, is expected complete by the end of 2026, per Elevate Quantum and the Denver Gazette (2025-08-29; re-searched 2026-07-06).
- **Golden, the school.** Colorado School of Mines, including its quantum-engineering program, plus clean-energy research at NLR (formerly NREL, on first reference). The lab was renamed by the Department of Energy on 2025-12-01, per energy.gov.
- **Denver, the metro anchor.** The population and business center that anchors the corridor's south end; not the research crown, which is Boulder.

## Industry figures (attribute, do not state bare)

- The **Colorado Photonics Industry Association** has cited on the order of 250 photonics companies and roughly 8,000 workers in the state. Always present these as the association's figures, and re-confirm the current numbers before publishing. Re-confirmed on the association's site 2026-07-06.
- The **Elevate Quantum** regional hub carries a $40.5 million EDA Tech Hubs award (July 2024), per the EDA. State matches from Colorado ($77 million) and New Mexico ($10 million) bring the total to about $127.5 million, per Elevate Quantum (searched 2026-07-06). State dollar figures only with a current, named source; the earlier "hundreds of millions" phrasing overstated the committed total and is retired.

## Energy and fusion (the light-based slice)

- **Solar** is the light-as-energy story for the corridor. Detail and sourcing are in `energy_why_and_how.md`.
- **Laser (inertial) fusion** is the corridor-relevant fusion path. Colorado's laser-fusion work is the in-thesis line; Xcimer in Denver announced the start of operations for its Phoenix laser in June 2026. Magnetic-confinement fusion is not a Colorado in-state strength. The large laser-fusion facility at CSU in Fort Collins (ATLAS) sits outside the corridor. Keep those distinctions honest.

## Guardrails (repeat on every external piece)

Operating versus opening-2026 versus horizon must stay distinct. NLR is "formerly NREL" on first reference. Photonic compute is emerging, an inference-first bet for the back half of the decade, not shipping at scale today. Never crown Denver as the research center; that is Boulder.
