# Photon Mountain · Context

## The idea

Silicon Valley was named for silicon, the material the last computing era was built on. The next era is being built on light. Photonic chips compute and move data with photons instead of electrons, at a fraction of the energy and heat. Solar turns light into power. Lasers can drive fusion. Sensors read the world with light. Colorado's Front Range already does this work at a level few places match, and it has no name. **Photon Mountain** is that name: a place brand for the corridor that builds the future of computing with light.

## Why Colorado, why this corridor

The core is **Boulder, Golden, Arvada, Denver**, with a greater ring out to Longmont. The corridor's real assets cluster here: light science and metrology in Boulder (NIST, JILA, CU), an operating photonic-chip cleanroom, and the photonic-integrated-circuit fab in Arvada. Golden holds the School of Mines; Denver anchors the metro end. The detailed roster, with honesty guardrails on what is operating today versus opening soon, is in `02_substance/corridor_facts_and_sources.md` and the comparison-map README.

## What Photon Mountain is and is not

- It **is** a place and ecosystem identity focused on what the corridor builds with light, energy, and compute (scope broadened from light-only 2026-07-05, per `CLAUDE.md`). Chips and interconnects, sensors and fiber optics, solar, and laser fusion lead the list. Quantum counts only as its light-based slice (optics and the photonic-IC fab). Energy generation, storage, grid, and cooling count when they power the computing corridor. Wind and water are noted where they co-locate but are not the focus.
- It **is not** the Colorado AI movement. Colorado AI is the separate statewide AI policy project this was spun out of on 2026-06-22. Colorado AI keeps the statewide policy work and its own copy of the compute substance. Photon Mountain does not use Colorado AI's motto, pillars, logo, or palette.
