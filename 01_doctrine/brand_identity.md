## 0. Origin

### The concept the identity expresses

The name says it: **light, and a mountain.** A photon is the particle of light; the Front Range is the mountain. The identity holds both. It is a place brand for a real corridor, and the deliberate counterpoint to "Silicon Valley", so it is confident and a little iconic.

### Zero carryover (a standing rule)

Photon Mountain deliberately did **not** inherit Colorado AI's look. Not the turquoise, pine green, and flag gold palette. Not the inverted-flag logo, nor the IBM Plex, Work Sans, and JetBrains Mono type. Not the motto, and not the three-pillar framing. The locked identity is a fresh build with zero carryover. This is a rule, not a one-time act: any deliverable found carrying Colorado AI's palette, fonts, motto, or pillars is a defect to fix, not a style choice.

### How this identity was made

Strategy first, pixels last, per the place-branding research in `branding_best_practices.md`. The sequence run in 2026: foundations, then brand strategy in words (audience, positioning, personality, essence). Then verbal identity (tone, lexicon, boilerplate, tagline), then visual identity (mark, palette, type). Then governance, and last the application to the deliverable pipeline.

The mark came from a four-concept review on 2026-07-05: Summit Sun, First Light, Ridge Wave, and Light Chevron. Paul picked Summit Sun. The three losing explorations and the review page were removed on 2026-09-22 under the project's no-hoarding rule; this paragraph is the decision record. The approved Summit Sun drafts remain in `brand_assets/`.

Application closed on 2026-09-22, when `../04_tools/brand_tokens.py` was built from the token block in section 3 and both deliverable build scripts were re-skinned to import from it. No build script hardcodes a brand value any more.

## 1. Strategy

### Audience (ranked)

1. **Builders.** The people who want to build anything in photons, energy, and compute: hardware founders, researchers, engineers, and the students becoming them. The brand exists to inspire them, show them the corridor's resources, and make them want to build here.
2. Investors and economic-development bodies.
3. State and local policymakers and corridor institutions.
4. Corridor residents and the general public.

A brand that speaks to everyone speaks to no one. Every piece leads with the builder; the other audiences read over the builder's shoulder.

### Positioning statement

For the builders of the light era, Photon Mountain is the Front Range corridor where the next computing era is under construction, because the pieces a builder needs are here. The proof clauses:

- Light science and metrology in Boulder (NIST, JILA, CU).
- An operating photonic-chip cleanroom at NIST Boulder.
- A photonic-integrated-circuit fab planned for Arvada, targeted for 2026 (Elevate Quantum).
- The Colorado School of Mines in Golden; clean-energy research at NLR (formerly NREL).
- Denver as the metro anchor.

Proof and status framing for every clause: `02_substance/corridor_facts_and_sources.md`. Fab status re-verified 2026-07-05 (Elevate Quantum; Denver Gazette, 2025-08-29): first phase, including the fab, expected complete by the end of 2026.

### Essence

**Build with light.**

Three words that hold the whole idea: light is the tool, and the corridor is where you build with it. The essence is the master tagline (section 1, Tagline) on purpose; one core phrase, used everywhere, compounds. (Updated from "Build on light" when the tagline locked, 2026-07-05.)

### Personality

Three committed traits (Aaker's competence and ruggedness, plus a third the project chose):

- **Competence.** The science is real and measured. The honesty doctrine is the proof.
- **Ruggedness.** The Rockies, a frontier, work done at altitude. The mountain half of the name.
- **Inspiration.** The brand shows the vision of the light era and invites the builder into it. Inspiration here means showing, never hyping: name the lab, the machine, the wavelength, and let the real thing do the exciting.

We are: precise, grounded, inviting, a little iconic, confident without volume.
We are not: boosterish, vague, breathless, derivative ("the Silicon Valley of X"), or louder than our facts.

### Pillars

Four proof areas, matching the corridor scope in `CLAUDE.md`. Attribution and status framing for each live in `02_substance/corridor_facts_and_sources.md`; re-verify before external use.

1. **Photonic chips and interconnects.** Proof: the operating photonic-chip cleanroom at NIST Boulder; the Arvada photonic-integrated-circuit fab, planned and targeted for 2026 (Elevate Quantum).
2. **Photonic sensors and fiber.** Proof: Boulder's metrology anchors, including NIST and JILA and the optical frequency comb.
3. **Light as energy: solar and laser fusion.** Proof: clean-energy research at NLR (formerly NREL) in Golden; laser inertial fusion work in the Denver area (for example Xcimer).
4. **Energy for compute.** Proof: generation, storage, and grid research that powers the computing corridor, anchored by NLR (formerly NREL).

Photonic quantum counts only as the light-based slice (quantum optics and the photonic-IC fab). The broad quantum story belongs to Colorado AI.

### Narrative (the spine)

Silicon Valley was named for the material of the last computing era. Photon Mountain is named for the medium of the next one: light. On Colorado's Front Range, between Boulder and Denver, the light era is already under construction. Today, NIST and JILA in Boulder run some of the most precise light science on Earth. An operating photonic-chip cleanroom sits at NIST Boulder. A photonic-integrated-circuit fab is planned for Arvada, targeted for 2026 (Elevate Quantum). On the horizon, photonic compute is emerging as an inference-first bet for the back half of the decade. It is not shipping at scale today, and the corridor says so plainly. That honesty is the brand. If you want to build with photons, energy, and compute, the mountain is where the pieces already are. Come build with light.

### Tagline (locked 2026-07-05: a two-level system)

- **Master tagline: "Build with light."** The permanent line. Goes everywhere: lockups, footers, social cards. Identical to the essence.
- **Supporting line: "The era of intelligence, built with light."** For heroes, openers, and long-form only. It declares the corridor's bet; nearby copy keeps the operating / opening-2026 / horizon framing honest.

The pair works because of the echo: the same three words in imperative and participle form. Never use both lines in the same small space. Practice basis: a tagline should be short and permanent, with longer lines serving underneath it (KickCharge Creative; Brand Master Academy; Lippincott).

## 2. Verbal identity

### Tone (the layer on top of voice.md)

The mechanical rules in `voice.md` stay underneath everything: no em dashes, one idea per sentence, attribute or label as goal. On top of them, the locked tone:

- **Inspire by showing.** Vision is welcome, hype is not. Every inspiring sentence points at something real: a lab, an instrument, a wavelength, a groundbreaking.
- **Speak to the builder.** Imperative mood is on-brand: build, come, measure, make. Address the reader as someone capable.
- **Warm-technical.** Technical enough to earn a researcher's respect, warm enough to feel like an invitation. When in doubt, cut adjectives and add a concrete noun.
- **Horizon statements are allowed, labeled.** The three-frame habit (operating today, opening 2026, on the horizon) is a tone feature, not just a compliance rule. Naming the frame is what makes the vision credible.

### Lexicon

Say: the corridor; the anchors; the fab (Arvada, once first reference is made); photonic compute is emerging; build with light. Also say: the light era; the era of intelligence; formerly NREL (first reference to NLR).
Avoid: revolutionary; world-class (unattributed); hub (as a bare noun); "the Silicon Valley of X" in any form; game-changing; unattributed superlatives; any verb that promises more than the sourced status.

### Boilerplate

**25 words.** Photon Mountain is Colorado's Front Range corridor for computing with light: photonic chips, sensors, solar, and laser fusion, anchored by Boulder, Golden, Arvada, and Denver.

**50 words.** Photon Mountain is Colorado's Front Range corridor for computing with light. Boulder holds NIST, JILA, CU, and an operating photonic-chip cleanroom. A photonic-integrated-circuit fab is planned for Arvada, targeted for 2026. Golden holds Mines and NLR (formerly NREL). Denver anchors the metro. If you build with light, start here.

**100 words.** Silicon Valley was named for the material of the last computing era. Photon Mountain is named for the medium of the next one: light. It is Colorado's Front Range corridor for computing with light, running Boulder, Golden, Arvada, Denver, with a greater ring out to Longmont. Today, Boulder's NIST and JILA run world-leading light science, and an operating photonic-chip cleanroom sits at NIST Boulder. A photonic-integrated-circuit fab is planned for Arvada, targeted for 2026 (Elevate Quantum). Photonic compute itself is emerging, not shipping at scale, and the corridor says so. The work spans chips, sensors, solar, and laser fusion. Come build with light.

(A note on "world-leading" in the 100-word boilerplate: the named instruments carry it. If it reads as a bare superlative in context, swap in "some of the most precise light science on Earth". That phrasing is the frequency-comb claim from `corridor_facts_and_sources.md`.)

### Naming conventions

- The name is **Photon Mountain**: two words, both capitalized, never hyphenated, never abbreviated in external copy (no "PM," no "PhoMo").
- Programs and sub-efforts are named under the master brand: "Photon Mountain [Program]". No sub-brand gets its own logo in v1.
- The domains are photonmountain.com and photonmountain.org (registered 2026-06-23).

## 3. Visual identity

### The mark: Summit Sun (locked 2026-07-05)

Two shapes: **the peak in front of a rising light.** A solid alpenglow disc rises behind an isosceles summit, the dawn every Front Range builder knows. It says the whole name without a letter: the light, the mountain. Paul picked it from a four-concept review (record in `brand_assets/mark_review.html`).

Construction spec (for the asset cut and for PT-6, from the approved draft):

- Disc: a solid circle, the rising light.
- Peak: an isosceles triangle in front of the disc, base about 1.27 times the disc's diameter, apex on the disc's vertical center line.
- The apex sits just above the disc's center (about a quarter radius above it). The disc's top rim stays clearly visible above the apex.
- The peak hides the disc's lower rim; the disc never touches the triangle's baseline edge-to-edge.
- Clear space: half the disc's diameter on all sides. Minimum size: 16 px height on screen.
- Full-color: Alpenglow disc with Snowfield peak on dark surfaces; Alpenglow disc with Summit Night peak on light surfaces.
- One-color rule: disc becomes an outline, peak stays solid, both in one palette color.
- What it is not: no rays, no gradients in v1, no prism or refracted beam, never inverted (the peak is always in front).

Approved draft SVGs live at `01_doctrine/brand_assets/` (`concept_d_summit_sun.svg` plus the one-color variant). The explorations (First Light, Ridge Wave, Light Chevron) stay in the folder as the decision record. Final asset files (SVG master, PNG exports, favicon) are cut at the start of PT-6; nothing external-facing ships without Paul's sign-off.

### Wordmark

"Photon Mountain" set in **Space Grotesk Medium**, both words initial-capped, tracking a touch tighter than default, single line. PT-6 picks the exact tracking value when the wordmark is cut. Lockups: horizontal (mark at left, wordmark at right, mark height equal to cap height times 1.6) and stacked (mark centered above wordmark). Wordmark-only is allowed where the mark has already appeared.

### Palette

Light-derived and locally true: alpenglow on the peaks at dawn, a violet laser line, the night sky over the summit, snowfield, granite. No turquoise, no pine green, no flag gold anywhere (Colorado AI carryover is prohibited).

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Summit Night | `#131A33` | Primary. Dark surfaces, body text on light. |
| Snowfield | `#F6F7FB` | Light surface. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Paper. |
| Granite | `#545B6B` | Secondary text, rules, captions. |
| Alpenglow | `#FF5A36` | Signature accent. Graphic use on light; accent text on dark. |
| Alpenglow Deep | `#C43C1C` | Alpenglow as text on light surfaces. |
| Laser Violet | `#7B5CFF` | Second accent. Graphic use; large text only on white. |
| Violet Bright | `#8F75FF` | Violet as text on dark surfaces. |
| Violet Deep | `#5636D6` | Violet as text on light surfaces. |
| Dawn Tint | `#FFE9E2` | Soft warm background tint. |

Contrast, computed 2026-07-05 (WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance method):

- Body text: Summit Night on White 17.2, on Snowfield 16.0, on Dawn Tint 14.7 (AA).
- Secondary text: Granite on White 6.8, on Snowfield 6.4 (AA).
- On dark: White on Summit Night 17.2; Alpenglow on Summit Night 5.5; Violet Bright on Summit Night 5.0 (AA).
- Accent text on light: Alpenglow Deep on White 5.2; Violet Deep on White 7.3 (AA).
- Graphic-or-large-only: Alpenglow on White 3.1; Laser Violet on White 4.4 (pass AA for large text only; never body text).

Usage rules: Summit Night and the neutrals carry most of every layout. Alpenglow is the one loud thing per piece. Laser Violet is the supporting accent for data, links, and secondary highlights. Never set body text in an accent color.

### Typography

All three families are open-source (SIL Open Font License), on Google Fonts, and embed in PDF builds. None overlaps Colorado AI's set (IBM Plex, Work Sans, JetBrains Mono).

| Role | Family | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display and headlines | Space Grotesk | Medium 500, Bold 700 |
| Body and UI | Inter | Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600 |
| Data, figures, code | Space Mono | Regular 400, Bold 700 |

Space Grotesk is a proportional relative of Space Mono, so display and data feel like one family. Type scale for PT-6: display 40 to 64, headline 24 to 32, body 10.5 to 12 (print points) or 16 to 18 (web px), caption in Granite.

### Supporting system

- Iconography: geometric line icons, 2 px stroke, rounded joins, single color from the palette.
- Photography and illustration: real corridor places and instruments over stock; night-and-light imagery (labs, ridgelines at dawn) fits the palette. No generic circuit-board art.
- Layout: generous margins, one Alpenglow moment per piece, Summit Night or White fields, rules in Granite.
