# Voice

The mechanical and honesty rules below carried over from Colorado AI and stay in force. The tone layer was locked with the identity on 2026-07-05; the full version, with the lexicon and boilerplate, is in `brand_identity.md`.

## Mechanical rules

- **No em dashes, ever.** Use commas, semicolons, periods, or parentheses.
- One idea per sentence. Say it once. Plain and direct over clever.
- Affirmative framing. Describe what the corridor builds and can build, not what it lacks.

## Honesty (non-negotiable, this is the brand's credibility)

- **Do not invent** figures, institutions, dates, or quotes.
- **Search before stating** any current status (who operates what, what opened, what a program funds). The facts move; do not trust memory.
- **Attribute every figure** to a credible source, or label it a goal.
- Keep the corridor guardrails. An operating fab is not one opening in 2026. NLR is "formerly NREL" on first reference. The Colorado Photonics Industry Association figures are the association's figures. Photonic compute is emerging, not shipping at scale.

## Tone (locked 2026-07-05 with the identity)

Confident and concrete. The corridor is real and the story is true, so the writing does not need to oversell. On top of that base, four locked habits from `brand_identity.md`:

- **Inspire by showing.** Vision is welcome, hype is not; every inspiring sentence points at something real (a lab, an instrument, a groundbreaking).
- **Speak to the builder.** Imperative mood is on-brand: build, come, measure, make.
- **Warm-technical.** Technical enough for a researcher, warm enough to be an invitation. When in doubt, cut adjectives and add a concrete noun.
- **Horizon statements are allowed, labeled.** The operating / opening-2026 / horizon frame is a tone feature, not just compliance.

The lexicon (words to use, words to avoid) and the approved boilerplate live in `brand_identity.md`.
