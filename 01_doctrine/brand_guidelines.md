# Photon Mountain · Brand guidelines (v1)
*How to apply the Photon Mountain brand, written so a designer or partner can work without the steward in the room. Created 2026-07-05. The strategy behind these rules lives in `brand_identity.md`, which stays canonical: if the two files disagree, the identity doc wins and this file gets fixed.*

**Status: internal until sign-off for external use.** The identity decisions are locked (tagline and mark included, 2026-07-05); final polished assets get cut at PT-6.

---

## 1. What Photon Mountain is

Photon Mountain is Colorado's Front Range corridor for computing with light: photonic chips, sensors, solar, and laser fusion, anchored by Boulder, Golden, Arvada, and Denver.

Silicon Valley was named for the material of the last computing era. Photon Mountain is named for the medium of the next one. The brand exists to inspire builders: the founders, researchers, and engineers who work in photons, energy, and compute. The full boilerplate set (25, 50, and 100 words) is in `brand_identity.md` section 2.

**Essence and master tagline: Build with light.**

The tagline is a two-level system. The master line, "Build with light.", goes everywhere: lockups, footers, social cards. The supporting line, "The era of intelligence, built with light.", is for heroes, openers, and long-form only. Never use both in the same small space.

## 2. Voice

Full conventions in `voice.md`; the lexicon and tone layer in `brand_identity.md`. The rules that settle most arguments:

- No em dashes, ever. Use commas, semicolons, periods, or parentheses.
- One idea per sentence. Plain and direct over clever.
- Attribute every figure to a named source, or label it a goal. Never invent.
- Frame time honestly: operating today, opening in 2026, or on the horizon, and say which.
- Inspire by showing. Name the lab, the instrument, the groundbreaking; cut the adjectives.
- Speak to the builder. Imperative mood is on-brand: build, come, measure, make.
- Never write "the Silicon Valley of X" in any form.

## 3. The name

- Write **Photon Mountain**: two words, both capitalized, never hyphenated.
- Do not abbreviate in external copy: no "PM," no "PhoMo."
- Programs are named under the master brand: "Photon Mountain [Program]." No sub-brand gets its own logo in v1.
- Domains: photonmountain.com and photonmountain.org.

## 4. The mark: Summit Sun (locked 2026-07-05)

The mark is the peak in front of a rising light: a solid alpenglow disc behind an isosceles summit. Approved draft SVGs are in `brand_assets/` (`concept_d_summit_sun.svg` and the one-color variant); final polished assets come from PT-6.

Construction rules, which final assets must follow:

- Disc: a solid circle, the rising light.
- Peak: an isosceles triangle in front, base about 1.27 times the disc's diameter, apex on the disc's vertical center line, just above the disc's center.
- The disc's top rim stays clearly visible above the apex; the peak hides the disc's lower rim.
- Clear space: half the disc's diameter on all sides. Minimum size: 16 px tall on screen.
- Full-color: Alpenglow disc with Snowfield peak on dark; Alpenglow disc with Summit Night peak on light.
- One-color rule: disc becomes an outline, peak stays solid, both in one palette color.

Never: add rays, add gradients, add a prism or refracted beam, tilt the mark, put the peak behind the disc, or recolor outside the palette.

## 5. The wordmark

"Photon Mountain" in Space Grotesk Medium, both words initial-capped, single line, tracking a touch tighter than default (exact value set when the final wordmark is cut). Lockups: horizontal (mark left, wordmark right, mark height 1.6 times cap height) and stacked (mark centered above). Wordmark-only is fine where the mark already appeared.

## 6. Color

The palette is light-derived and locally true: alpenglow on the peaks at dawn, a violet laser line, the night sky over the summit, snowfield, granite.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Summit Night | `#131A33` | Primary. Dark surfaces, body text on light. |
| Snowfield | `#F6F7FB` | Light surface. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Paper. |
| Granite | `#545B6B` | Secondary text, rules, captions. |
| Alpenglow | `#FF5A36` | Signature accent. Graphic use on light; accent text on dark. |
| Alpenglow Deep | `#C43C1C` | Alpenglow as text on light surfaces. |
| Laser Violet | `#7B5CFF` | Second accent. Graphic use; large text only on white. |
| Violet Bright | `#8F75FF` | Violet as text on dark surfaces. |
| Violet Deep | `#5636D6` | Violet as text on light surfaces. |
| Dawn Tint | `#FFE9E2` | Soft warm background tint. |

Pairing rules (contrast computed 2026-07-05, WCAG 2.1 method; ratios in `brand_identity.md`):

- Body text is always Summit Night on White, Snowfield, or Dawn Tint, or White/Snowfield on Summit Night.
- Secondary text is Granite on light surfaces.
- Accent text on light surfaces uses the Deep variants (Alpenglow Deep, Violet Deep).
- Accent text on Summit Night uses Alpenglow or Violet Bright.
- Alpenglow `#FF5A36` and Laser Violet `#7B5CFF` on white: graphics and large text only, never body text.
- One Alpenglow moment per piece. Alpenglow is the loud thing; everything else stays quiet.

Never: use turquoise, pine green, or flag gold (the Colorado AI palette is prohibited here); set body text in any accent color; place Alpenglow text on Dawn Tint.

## 7. Typography

All families are open-source (SIL Open Font License) and on Google Fonts, so any partner can install them free.

| Role | Family | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display and headlines | Space Grotesk | Medium 500, Bold 700 |
| Body and UI | Inter | Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600 |
| Data, figures, code | Space Mono | Regular 400, Bold 700 |

Scale guidance: display 40 to 64, headline 24 to 32, body 10.5 to 12 (print points) or 16 to 18 (web px), captions in Granite. Never substitute look-alike fonts, and never use IBM Plex, Work Sans, or JetBrains Mono (the Colorado AI set).

## 8. Imagery and iconography

- Photography: real corridor places and instruments over stock. Night-and-light imagery (labs, laser lines, ridgelines at dawn) fits the palette.
- No generic circuit-board art, no AI-slop cityscapes, no stock handshakes.
- Iconography: geometric line icons, 2 px stroke, rounded joins, one palette color at a time.
- Every image of a real facility must match its honest status: do not picture a rendering as a built thing without a label.

## 9. Layout

Generous margins. Summit Night or White fields. Rules and captions in Granite. One Alpenglow moment per piece. Data in Space Mono. Attribution line on any piece that states a figure.

## 10. Correct and incorrect

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|
| "An operating photonic-chip cleanroom sits at NIST Boulder." | "The corridor's fabs are shipping photonic processors at scale." |
| "A photonic-integrated-circuit fab opens in Arvada in 2026 (Elevate Quantum)." | "Arvada's operating fab..." |
| "NLR (formerly NREL)" on first reference. | "NREL" or bare "NLR" on first reference. |
| "The Colorado Photonics Industry Association cites roughly 250 photonics companies statewide." | "Colorado has 250 photonics companies." |
| The mark in one palette color, clear space respected. | The mark with a gradient, rays, or a prism beam. |
| "Come build with light." | "Join the revolution!" |

## 11. Using the brand (usage policy, v1)

- No third party may use the Photon Mountain name or mark as a brand without Paul's written approval.
- Corridor institutions and press may reference the name in text freely.
- Partners producing material under an approval follow this document exactly; when in doubt, ask the steward.
- The steward is Paul; brand decisions and changes are recorded in `brand_identity.md`'s changelog.
