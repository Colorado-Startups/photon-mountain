# Trademarks and brand usage

The content in this repository is openly licensed (see `LICENSING.md`). The brand is not. No content license grants trademark rights, so this file says what is reserved and what you may do anyway.

## What is reserved

- The name **Photon Mountain**
- The **Photon Mountain wordmark**
- The **Summit Sun** mark, in every color variant
- The visual identity as a system: the Summit Night, Alpenglow, and Laser Violet palette together with the Space Grotesk, Inter, and Space Mono type roles, used as Photon Mountain's identity

These are held by Colorado Startups. This is the standing usage policy recorded in `01_doctrine/brand_identity.md` section 4.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the corridor by name.** Write about Photon Mountain, quote it, cite it, criticize it, report on it. Referential use is not restricted, and it is the point of a place brand.
- **Say you are in it.** A company, lab, or institution in the corridor may say so plainly: "based in Photon Mountain", "part of Colorado's Photon Mountain corridor". That is a statement of fact about location.
- **Reuse the substance and the maps** under their licenses, with attribution, including in commercial work.
- **Reproduce a deliverable** (an infographic, a map image) unmodified, with its attribution intact.

## What needs written approval

- Using the name or the Summit Sun mark **as your own brand**, or as part of one: in a product name, company name, logo, or domain.
- Any use that implies endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or membership where none exists.
- Modifying the mark, or rebuilding it in a different palette, and presenting the result as Photon Mountain's identity.
- Merchandise or promotional goods carrying the name or mark.

Ask Paul at Colorado Startups.

## The reasoning, since it is not obvious

A place brand's only real asset is that people trust what it says. The honesty guardrails in `02_substance/` exist because one oversold claim discredits the whole corridor, and the corridor's credibility belongs to every institution in it, not just to this project.

That is why the split is this way round. The **story** is open, because a place brand grows by being used and repeated. The **mark** is held, because a mark anyone can apply to anything cannot signal that the guardrails were followed. Guard the rules, not the assets.

Place-branding practice supports the split. The strongest modern place brands publish their system as a usable toolkit with clear rules rather than locking it behind a gatekeeper; see practice 6 in `01_doctrine/branding_best_practices.md`.

## Expected to change

This is the v1 policy, written before the first outside request arrived. The governance note in `brand_identity.md` says to revisit when one does. The case record in `branding_best_practices.md` points the same way: founder-held now, community stewardship later, once there is traction.
