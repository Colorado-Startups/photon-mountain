# Photon Mountain

The identity for Colorado's computing-with-light corridor: the Front Range from Boulder through Golden, Arvada, and Denver, where the state can build the future of computing on the photon. The deliberate answer to "Silicon Valley," named for the medium of the next computing era rather than the material of the last.

Focus: photonics. Photonic chips (processors and optical interconnects), photonic sensors, solar, and laser fusion energy, plus the light-based slice of quantum (quantum optics and the corridor's photonic-integrated-circuit fab).

Spun out of the Colorado AI project on 2026-06-22. Colorado AI remains the statewide AI policy movement; Photon Mountain is the corridor place brand. Start with `CLAUDE.md` for project rules, then `project_context.md`.

Status: day one. Deliverables moved in and rendering; substance docs are seeds from Colorado AI pending a reframing pass; the brand identity is not yet designed; the GitHub repo is not yet created.
