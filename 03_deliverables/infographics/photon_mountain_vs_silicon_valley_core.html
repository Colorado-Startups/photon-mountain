This is the comparison that counts. Strip away the suburbs and two cores are left, drawn at the same scale, each anchored by a major city. Silicon Valley has San Jose. Photon Mountain has Denver, with Boulder, Golden, and Arvada around it. The dots are scaled to population, so the anchors read at a glance. Colorado already has a core and a city to anchor it. The work ahead is to fill in the density between.
