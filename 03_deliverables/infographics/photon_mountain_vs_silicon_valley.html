Read the cores against each other first. Each one is anchored by a major city, San Jose for the Valley and Denver for Photon Mountain, ringed by the towns where the labs and fabs sit. The dots are scaled to population, so the two anchors are the big circles you see first. Photon Mountain's core runs a little wider, Boulder down to Denver, because Denver anchors it the way San Jose anchors the Valley. A bigger ring is not a better region, and the outlines hide something. Silicon Valley wraps the south end of San Francisco Bay, so part of its footprint is open water with no one on it. Photon Mountain is solid Front Range. The measure that matters is people, not acres, and Colorado already has the core and the city to anchor it.

Why Photon Mountain. A photon is the quantum particle of light, so one word holds both the photonic chips Colorado can manufacture and the quantum science Colorado can run. It names a real thing the way silicon did. Colorado's answer to the Valley.
