Photon Mountain

Silicon Valley was named for the material of the last era. Photon Mountain is named for the medium of the next, light. Along Colorado’s Front Range, one corridor is building the AI age out of photons: light-based chips, renewable energy, and quantum systems.

What is Photon Mountain

Photon Mountain is a real place, the Colorado Front Range corridor where the AI age gets built out of light. A photon is the particle of light, so one name holds both halves of the work: the photonic chips Colorado can manufacture and the quantum science it can run. It is two rings, a dense core of labs and fabs and a greater ring of cities around it.

Core The dense cluster of Boulder, Golden, Arvada, and Denver. The labs, the fabs, and the metro hub of the corridor.
